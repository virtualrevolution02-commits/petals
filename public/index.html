Privacy Policy for Petals

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Petals app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by you.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application requires you to log in using Google Sign-In. We collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Your Name and Email address (provided by Google) to uniquely identify you and display your name to your partner.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos and captions ("Moments") that you upload to share with your connected partner.

2. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage). All data is encrypted in transit. Your moments are only visible to you and the specific partner you have connected with via a secure pairing code.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

4. Data Deletion

You can log out of the app at any time, which clears your local session. To request complete deletion of your account and uploaded photos from our servers, you may contact the developer directly.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
